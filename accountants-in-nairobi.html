What We Do

Accounting Services Tailored to Your Business

APSIS Business Consulting Ltd delivers accounting services that go beyond compliance. We maintain your financial records with precision, prepare reports that inform decision-making, and ensure regulatory adherence — so you can focus on growing your business.

Core Accounting Services

Bookkeeping & Financial Record Management

We maintain detailed, audit-ready financial records for your business — from daily transaction recording to ledger reconciliation. Our bookkeeping ensures transparency, accuracy, and a complete audit trail for every transaction, giving you confidence in your numbers.

Financial Analysis & Reporting

Beyond recording numbers, we deliver analytical insights into your business performance. Our financial analysis empowers data-driven decision-making and strategic planning, helping you understand profitability drivers, cash flow patterns, and growth opportunities.

Regulatory Compliance

We help companies adhere to Kenyan tax laws and accounting standards, preventing compliance issues before they arise. From statutory reporting requirements to KRA filing obligations, we ensure your business stays on the right side of regulation.

Financial Statement Preparation

We prepare compliant financial statements in accordance with IFRS and Kenyan reporting standards — including statements of financial position, comprehensive income, cash flows, and changes in equity — ready for audit, board review, or regulatory submission.
