What this estimate assumes

  • A resident employee with one employer, so the full personal relief of KES 2,400 a month applies.
  • NSSF, SHIF and the housing levy are all worked out on the gross pay entered. If your contract or your employer treats some allowances differently, your payslip can differ.
  • Pension deduction is capped at KES 30,000 (NSSF plus extra contributions combined). Post-retirement medical fund deduction is capped at KES 15,000. Mortgage interest deduction is capped at KES 30,000. Insurance relief is 15% of premiums up to KES 5,000.
  • The disability exemption is not included.
  • PAYE bands and personal relief are unchanged by the Finance Act 2026. NSSF uses the Year 4 limits that took effect in February 2026.

Common questions

Is PAYE charged on gross pay?

No. NSSF, SHIF and the employee's housing levy are deducted first, and PAYE is charged on the pay that remains. This has applied since 27 December 2024.

Why is there no PAYE on the first KES 24,000 of taxable pay?

Tax on the first KES 24,000 is KES 2,400 at 10%, which equals the monthly personal relief for residents. For an employee with only the standard deductions, PAYE starts at a gross pay of about KES 26,741.

What is the most NSSF an employee pays?

KES 6,480 a month: 6% of pay up to KES 108,000, under the limits in force since February 2026. The employer pays the same amount.

Is SHIF capped?

No. It is 2.75% of gross pay with a minimum of KES 300 and no upper limit.

How much is the housing levy and who pays it?

1.5% of gross pay from the employee and 1.5% from the employer.

When must PAYE be paid?

Employers must pay PAYE and file the return by the 9th of the following month.
