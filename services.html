The Foundation

Our Core Services

Accountability

All businesses are required to prepare accounts in an acceptable format predetermined by accounting standards and national legislation. Whether you are a sole trader, close corporation, private limited, a government enterprise, a charity organisation or town council — statutory accounts are required for self-assessment and for preparation of annual reports.

Fiscal Responsibility

Revenue Authorities have gone high-tech to get all individuals, residents or otherwise, to pay their income tax as guided by the various Acts. Tax avoidance has therefore been addressed and various audit and settlements with the taxman are in the offing if not already there, for every taxpayer.

Statutory Obligations

Notwithstanding fiscal responsibility, organisations need to concentrate on managing and developing their business — doing what they are best at. It is therefore important to have someone they can rely on to help with tax, social security, VAT and financial reporting matters.
