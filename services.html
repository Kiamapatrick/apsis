Why it matters

Three obligations every organization carries, and where we step in.

Accountability

All businesses are required to prepare accounts in an acceptable format set by accounting standards and national legislation. Whether you are a sole trader, close corporation, private limited company, government enterprise, charity or town council, statutory accounts are required for self-assessment and for preparing annual reports.

Fiscal responsibility

Revenue authorities have gone high-tech to get all individuals, resident or otherwise, to pay their income tax as guided by the various Acts. Tax avoidance has therefore been addressed, and audits and settlements with the taxman are in the offing, if not already here, for every taxpayer.

Statutory obligations

Organizations need to concentrate on managing and developing their business, doing what they do best. It is important to have someone they can rely on for tax, social security, VAT and financial reporting matters.
