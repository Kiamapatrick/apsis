Quick Summary

  • KRA has published a notice listing items excluded from consolidated cargo clearance under the KES 2 million framework.
  • The notice is addressed to all cargo clearance stakeholders.
  • Items 12–30 may be allowed as consolidated cargo if presented together, with the combined volume capped at 5% of total cargo volume (CBM).

What KRA Announced

KRA notified all cargo clearance stakeholders that the listed items are excluded from consolidated cargo clearance for consignments under the KES 2 million framework, and advised stakeholders to take note of the exclusions and ensure compliance with the applicable customs clearance requirements.

The notice does not explain the KES 2 million framework itself or specify an effective date. For those details, refer to KRA directly.

Excluded Items That May Be Allowed as Consolidated Cargo Under Conditions (12–30)

The following items are excluded but may be allowed as consolidated cargo if presented together and the conditions below are met:

  1. Assorted foodstuff
  2. Complete kitchen
  3. Designer brands for finished manufactured products
  4. Plant and machinery, excluding small machinery e.g. handheld tractors and similar small-scale equipment that support the agricultural and small industry sectors
  5. Smart phones and watches
  6. Used clothes and shoes
  7. Wooden/Steel/Aluminium doors
  8. Televisions
  9. Car accessories - car audio systems/speakers, dash cameras, reversing cameras/parking sensors and GPS navigation devices
  10. Musical instruments, public address systems, entertainment equipment and related accessories
  11. Sanitary ware
  1. Cables
  2. Ready-made garments
  3. Fabrics
  4. Interlining material
  5. Beddings and household linen
  6. Carpets
  7. Cosmetics, toiletries and beauty products
  8. New motorcycle spare parts and accessories
  9. New shoes
  10. Terry towels
  11. Auto paints, epoxy and related products
  12. Padlocks
  13. Curtain accessories
  14. Door locks/handles
  15. Hardware items (machetes, meters, valves)
  16. Bathroom accessories (towel holders, towel bars, shower heads)
  17. Cosmetic and haircare products/henna
  18. Computer accessories
  19. Mobile phone accessories

Three Conditions for Items 12–30

For items 12–30, KRA states they will be subject to the following conditions:

Volume Cap

The combined volume of these items must not exceed 5% of the total cargo volume, measured in cubic metres (CBM).

Full Declaration

All of them must be fully declared on the invoice, the entry and the list of importers, with the description and quantity of each item.

Unit Values

They are subject to the applicable unit values, determined using the applicable customs valuation method.

Before You Ship

  • Check your shipment list against the notice.
  • Make sure descriptions and quantities on your invoice are complete.
  • Confirm the details with your clearing agent.
