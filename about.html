Who We Are

APSIS Business Consulting Ltd

Apsis Business Consulting Limited is a dedicated accounting and tax consulting firm, providing comprehensive fiscal solutions for businesses across all sectors and industries. In an era of heightened regulatory scrutiny and advanced compliance demands from the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA), we serve as your trusted partner to navigate complexity with confidence. We understand that your primary focus is on managing and developing your business. Our role is to handle the critical financial reporting and tax obligations that underpin your success — allowing you to concentrate on what you do best, secure in the knowledge that your fiscal matters are in expert hands.

Strength and Capabilities

At APSIS, we understand that complex financial challenges require specialized solutions. Our capability is rooted in our human resources — a dedicated team of professionals who are not just accountants, but specialists in their respective fields.

  • Taxation & Compliance: Navigating KRA regulations and ensuring full legal adherence.
  • Financial Reporting: Crafting compliant statutory accounts and insightful management reports.
  • Sector-Specific Finance: Applying deep knowledge of industries like manufacturing, retail, NGOs, and government.

By combining this specialized knowledge, we provide a powerful, unified approach to managing your financial ecosystem — delivering clarity, efficiency, and strategic advantage.
