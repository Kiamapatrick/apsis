Import & Export Review Services

Customs Valuation & Tariff Classification

We review your import declarations to ensure correct customs valuation methods (transaction value, deductive value, computed value) and accurate HS code classification. Misclassification leads to under/over-payment of duty, penalties, and clearance delays.

Advance Tax Rulings — Certainty Before You Commit

An advance tax ruling from KRA provides binding guidance on the tax treatment of a proposed cross-border transaction. This certainty is invaluable for pricing, contract negotiation, and risk management.

When to Seek an Advance Ruling
  • Complex import/export transactions with uncertain duty or VAT treatment
  • New product lines or markets where classification is ambiguous
  • Structuring cross-border services, royalties, or intellectual property licensing
  • Major capital equipment imports where valuation methodology is debatable
  • Transactions with related parties requiring transfer pricing alignment
Guidance
Our Advance Ruling Service
  • Pre-ruling assessment: we evaluate whether a ruling is necessary and likely to succeed
  • Application preparation: comprehensive submission with legal analysis, facts, and supporting documents
  • KRA engagement: we manage correspondence, clarifications, and follow-up until ruling issuance
  • Implementation guidance: translating the ruling into operational procedures for your team
  • Ongoing monitoring: tracking regulatory changes that may affect the ruling's validity

Frequently Asked Questions

An advance tax ruling is a binding written opinion from KRA on how a specific tax law applies to a proposed transaction. You need one when: (1) the tax treatment is genuinely uncertain, (2) the financial exposure is material, (3) you need certainty for pricing or contract terms, or (4) the transaction structure is novel. The ruling protects you from penalties if you act in accordance with it — even if KRA later changes its interpretation.

KRA typically issues advance rulings within 60 days of receiving a complete application. Complex cases involving transfer pricing or novel structures may take longer. APSIS prepares thorough applications to minimize KRA clarification requests and accelerate the timeline.
