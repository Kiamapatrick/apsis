Partnering with APSIS

We understand the role of a contractual engagement. We define the scope of services to be delivered, the terms and conditions, and the deadlines, whether for individuals or large corporations.

  • Scope of work

    Through whichever channel suits you (a call, WhatsApp or email), we agree in principle on the project process and deliverables. That includes an evaluation at every milestone, and constant communication from start to completion.

  • Professional fees

    Our fees are based on the scope of work. The complexity of a matter determines the level of skill and responsibility it needs, and the time it will take to resolve.

  • Acceptance

    We draw up terms and conditions that work for both sides, including fees and payment terms. Both parties confirm by signing the contract that endorses our engagement for each project.

How an engagement runs

Five steps, from the first conversation to the support that follows delivery.

  1. Initial consultation

    We discuss your needs and outline the right approach for your business.

  2. Scope and proposal

    We define the deliverables and issue a proforma invoice detailing all fees.

  3. Agreement signed

    Both parties sign the engagement contract, and the 50% deposit is received.

  4. Delivery and review

    Work is delivered with milestone check-ins. The final balance is settled on completion.

  5. Ongoing support

    We stay available for follow-up queries and continued advisory services.

How fees are billed

Our fees are based on the scope of work. The complexity of a matter determines the level of skill and responsibility we apply, and the time we spend resolving it.

We scope the work, then issue a proforma invoice showing the fees chargeable. A deposit of 50% is payable when the agreement is signed. The balance is payable in full when the assignment concludes.
