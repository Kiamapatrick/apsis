End-to-end VAT compliance for Kenyan businesses

VAT compliance in Kenya demands precision every month: filing by the 20th, reconciling eTIMS invoices, recovering input tax correctly, and staying audit-ready. APSIS manages the full cycle so you never miss a deadline or leave recoverable VAT on the table.

Monthly VAT return filing

We prepare and file your VAT returns on iTax by the 20th of every month, accurately and on time. Nil returns included. Our process includes pre-filing review of output tax, input tax claims and eTIMS reconciliation to prevent KRA queries.

eTIMS invoice reconciliation

Your eTIMS-generated invoices must match your VAT return. We reconcile cumulative eTIMS data against declared output tax monthly, eliminating the number one KRA audit trigger. Discrepancies are identified and resolved before filing.

Why proactive VAT compliance matters

Input tax = cash flow

Every shilling of valid input tax recovered is working capital. Missed claims directly reduce your liquidity.

eTIMS mismatches = audit risk

Frequently asked questions

What business owners ask us about VAT in Kenya.

Any business with annual taxable turnover of Ksh 5 million or more must register for VAT within 30 days of reaching the threshold. Voluntary registration is available below this threshold and can be beneficial if you regularly incur input VAT on purchases. Importers of taxable goods and businesses supplying government entities may also be required to register regardless of turnover.

Late filing attracts a minimum penalty of Ksh 10,000 per return. Late payment incurs interest of 1% per month on the outstanding VAT, compounded from the due date. Persistent non-compliance can lead to KRA enforcement action including bank levies and asset seizure.

Every VAT-registered business must issue invoices through eTIMS. KRA automatically cross-references your cumulative eTIMS invoice data against the output tax declared on your VAT return. Any mismatch triggers a query. APSIS reconciles both monthly before filing to ensure alignment.
