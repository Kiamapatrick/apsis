Financial advisory for growth-focused businesses

APSIS Business Consulting Ltd provides financial advisory that goes beyond compliance. We help business owners and leadership teams understand their numbers, model future scenarios, and make capital allocation decisions that drive sustainable growth.

Financial analysis & performance review

We deliver analytical insights into your business performance: profitability by segment, cost structure analysis, margin trends and cash flow drivers. Our reviews give you the clarity to make data-driven decisions rather than relying on intuition.

Analysis

Financial planning & analysis (FP&A)

Structured FP&A processes including budgeting, forecasting, variance analysis and scenario planning. We build financial models that connect operational drivers to financial outcomes, enabling proactive management rather than reactive firefighting.

FP&A

Financial modeling & forecasting

Custom-built models for business planning, fundraising, acquisition analysis and capital expenditure evaluation. Our models are transparent, auditable, and designed for stress-testing assumptions under multiple scenarios.

Modeling

Investment appraisal & capital allocation

Rigorous evaluation of investment opportunities using NPV, IRR, payback period and sensitivity analysis. We help you allocate capital to the highest-return opportunities while quantifying downside risk, which is essential for board-level decisions.

Investment

Budget management & cost control

Design and implementation of budgeting frameworks aligned to your strategic objectives. We establish variance reporting, cost centre accountability and rolling forecast processes that keep spending aligned with priorities.

Budgeting

Financial consulting & strategy

Advisory on financial strategy, capital structure optimization, financing decisions and business restructuring. We bring an independent perspective to complex financial choices, from debt vs equity to dividend policy and shareholder returns.
